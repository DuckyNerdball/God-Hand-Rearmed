# Sound effects

`style_switch.ogg`: the sound of a style switch in play (Moveset Presets > Signal Style Switch).

`launcher/`: the launcher's saloon (K's): `bar_ambience.ogg` (the bar's murmur, looped under the music),
`pen_circle.ogg` (the pencil loop drawn round a bill item), `smoothie_drink.ogg` (the smoothie's last sip).
