The saloon images in this folder
================================

These images (the launcher's saloon: its layers, door frames, Options zoom frames, the book and the snack sprites
and shadows) were rendered for God Hand: Rearmed from the 3D assets credited below. They are separate from the
God Hand: Rearmed code, which has its own licence (LICENSE at the top of the repository). They contain no game data.

Each file carries the licence its sources require:

- Files named cherries*  (the plate's cherries, their shadows)
    CC BY-SA 4.0  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
    Based on "Cherries" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cherries-4d406299153c455f94564b49b56bd6a9)
    by Carissa (https://sketchfab.com/cclarke468), licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

- Files named bananas*  (the banana bunch, its shadow)
    CC BY-NC-SA 4.0  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
    Based on "A Bunch of Bananas" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/a-bunch-of-bananas-df544cb8528e43a09510327643286161)
    by JoshElo (https://sketchfab.com/JoshElo), licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

- Every other file
    CC BY-NC-SA 4.0  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
    (c) God Hand: Rearmed. Some of these show part of the bananas (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, the same licence); none of
    them contains the cherries: the Options zoom frames ship with the plate's fruit removed, and the launcher draws
    each piece from its own file.
